For parkrunners who forget their printout

Your parkrun barcode, on your phone and watch.

No printout needed. Bibless opens straight to your barcode at full brightness, so you get scanned at the finish and get on with your coffee.

Get it on Google Play See how it works

For Android phones and Wear OS watches. Independent, and not affiliated with parkrun.

01 · Ready to scan

Barcode or QR code. Your call, per device.

Add your athlete ID once and the code is ready. Pick whichever your volunteers' scanner reads best, separately for the phone and the watch.

Phone
Watch

02 · Always scannable

Light or dark. The code stays black on white.

Dark mode or not, the code card never changes colour, so scanners read it first time. Bibless turns the brightness up to full and keeps the screen on until you've been scanned.

Theme

03 · The whole family

Everyone you run with, one tap apart.

Save barcodes for the whole family, drag them into your order, and swipe between them on your watch. Tap Me on the phone to open it.

04 · In sync

Add it on either device. It shows up on both.

Type a new athlete ID on your phone, or straight on your watch, and it appears on the other by itself. Leave the phone at home on run day.

05 · Faster than a printout

Opens straight to your barcode.

Have the app open on your first barcode, or skip the app altogether:

  • ▦Home screen widget
  • ◐Quick Settings tile, even on the lock screen
  • ↗App shortcuts
  • ◯Watch tile, one swipe from the watch face

06 · Yours alone

No account. No ads. No tracking.

Your barcodes stay on your devices. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sell. Read the privacy policy for Android or Apple.

Coming soon Community runs

Time your own run, from the same app.

Bibless is growing into a toolkit for free, timed community runs: a stopwatch on the phone and the watch, a scanner for barcodes and finish tokens, and a web app that combines everything into results. It all works offline, and every record is signed.
