Bibless Privacy Policy

For Android phone and Wear OS watch · Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Apple version

Bibless is a small app for storing parkrun barcodes and showing them on your Android phone and Wear OS watch. It is made by Nils-Börge Margotti ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes.

Short version: Bibless has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything you enter stays on your devices.

What data the app stores

When you add a barcode, Bibless stores:

  • the name you give it (for example "Me" or a family member's first name),
  • the parkrun athlete ID (for example A1234567),
  • your display settings (QR code or barcode, for phone and watch).

This data is stored locally on your phone and watch. Bibless does not ask for your email address, location, contacts, health data or any other personal information, and it does not use the camera.

Syncing between phone and watch

To keep your phone and watch up to date, Bibless sends the data listed above between your own devices using the Wearable Data Layer in Google Play services. Depending on how your devices are connected, Google may relay this data through its servers. That relay is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Bibless itself never sends your data to me or to any third party.

Backups

Android may include the app's data in your device backup (Android Backup in your Google account). These backups are controlled by your device settings and by Google's privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No crash reporting services
  • No sale or sharing of data
  • No network connections to servers operated by me

Deleting your data

Delete a barcode in the app to remove it from your phone and watch. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from that device.

Children

Bibless does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents can store their children's parkrun barcodes in the app. That data stays on the parent's devices, like all other data in the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, I will update this policy and change the date above.
