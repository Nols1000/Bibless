Bibless helps runners and volunteers at community runs. Keep your athlete barcode ready on your iPhone and Apple Watch, time finishers and scan barcodes at the finish line.

YOUR BARCODE, READY TO SCAN

• Add your athlete ID once and show it as a QR code or classic barcode
• Save barcodes for the whole family, arrange them in your order and switch between them
• Add barcodes on your phone or watch and keep them in sync
• Open straight to your barcode at full brightness, with the screen kept on while you show it
• Get to your barcode quickly with Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, Siri and the Action button

HELP AT THE FINISH LINE

• Time a run on your phone or watch: start the clock, then tap for each finisher
• Undo the last recorded finish if you tap by mistake
• Use your phone's camera to scan athlete barcodes and numbered finish tokens, or enter codes by hand
• Pair each athlete with a finish token, or record athletes in finish order without tokens
• Record finishers who have no barcode, with an optional name
• Scan the organiser's run QR code to join a run before recording, or record without joining

SHARE YOUR RECORDED SESSIONS

• Keep timer and scanner sessions saved on your phone
• Transfer stopped watch timer sessions to your paired phone
• Export sessions as CSV and JSON files and share them with the organiser

Organisers use the separate Bibless web app to set up community runs, print finish tokens and combine volunteer sessions into results.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS, NO TRACKING

Your barcodes and recorded sessions are stored on your devices. You choose when to export a session and who to share it with. Barcode display, timing and scanning work offline.

Supports parkrun athlete barcodes. Bibless is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by parkrun.
